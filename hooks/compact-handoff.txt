Produce the summary as a filled-out HANDOFF FORM. Be specific and detailed: keep exact file paths, function and variable names, commit hashes, commands run, and numbers. Do not drop small facts; when in doubt, keep it.

=== HANDOFF FORM ===
1. MISSION: what we are building and why (1-2 lines).
2. DONE: completed work, with file paths and commit hashes.
3. IN FLIGHT: the exact step in progress when compaction hit (file, function, what was about to happen).
4. NEXT STEPS: numbered, in order, specific enough to execute without re-reading old context.
5. FILES TOUCHED: full paths, with one line on what changed in each.
6. DECISIONS + CONSTRAINTS: choices the user made or approved this session, verbatim where possible.
7. GOTCHAS: anything tried that failed (and why), anything that must NOT be done.
8. OPEN QUESTIONS: anything waiting on the user (or "none").
=== END FORM ===

After the form, end the summary with this instruction: "CONTINUE WORKING immediately from NEXT STEPS. Do not greet, do not re-plan from scratch, do not ask permission to proceed."
